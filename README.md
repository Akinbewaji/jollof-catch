# Jollof Catch

A fast-paced arcade game built for [start.ng](https://start.ng).

## How to Play
- **Controls**: Slide left and right using touch/mouse drag, horizontal arrows, or `A` / `D` keys.
- **Goal**: Catch falling plates of delicious jollof rice (🍛), crispy chicken (🍗), and sweet plantains / dodo (🍌).
- **Special Drop**: Catch the chilled drink (🥤) to cool down 1 burn and earn +3 bonus plates!
- **The Golden Pot (🍲✨)**: A blazing fast falling pot (+5 instant plates & fanfare) tempting players into risky dashes!
- **Dodge**: Avoid the fiery scotch bonnet peppers / Ata Rodo (🌶️). 3 burns and the round ends (or just 1 in Wahala Mode)!
- **Wind Gusts (🌪️)**: Adapt to drifting wedding breezes that swerve items mid-air.
- **Pause**: Press `Esc` or tap the ⏸️ pause button at any time.

## Game Modes
- **🍗 Standard Party**: Casual owambe fun with 3 burns allowance, moderate speeds, and recovery chilled drinks.
- **🌶️ Wahala (Spicy Nightmare) Mode**: Pure arcade rage! 1 single burn means instant game over, 42% pepper hazard rate, blistering fall speeds, and aggressive wind gusts!

## Rage & Viral Features
- **Party Gossip / Game-Over Roasts**: Hilarious Nigerian party roasts every time you get burned out.
- **"Share Your Shame" Card**: One-tap copy button generating a hilarious roast and stats snippet ready to paste to WhatsApp, Discord, or X.
- **The Golden Pot Trap**: Tempting high-speed pot drops that lure players directly into descending peppers.
- **Drifting Breeze**: Shifting wind vectors causing items to curve away at the last second.

## Features & Integration
- **Instructions & Guide**: Built-in How to Play modal with item breakdown and gameplay tips.
- **Custom Settings**: Real-time Web Audio synthesizer, haptics, particle effects, and control responsiveness (Smooth vs Snappy).
- **Start.ng Integration**: Dynamic character customization reflecting the player's start.ng avatar, skin tone, hair style, clothing colors, hat, and accessories with shared global leaderboard tracking.
- **100% Static & Standalone**: Pure HTML5 Canvas, modern CSS, and JavaScript with relative paths and zero external libraries or network CDNs.
